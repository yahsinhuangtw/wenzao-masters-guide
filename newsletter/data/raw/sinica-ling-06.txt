摘要
 In this talk, I discuss a selected list of Kra-dai and Austronesian lexical comparisons that I consider to have descended from the common ancestor— Austro-Tai. During the past decades, I have presented evidence of phonological correspondences to support the validity of the comparisons. In this presentation, I will elaborate on what may be inferred from these shared vocabulary items about the cultural history of the Austro-Tai people. Discussions include issues concerning the homeland, agricultural dispersal, wild and domesticated animals, kinships and others. Early language contact between Kra-Dai and other ethnic groups in South China and adjacent Southeast Asian areas is noted when relevant.
