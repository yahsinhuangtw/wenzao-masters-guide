## Student experience: missing the first round

A master's student reported missing the first round of course selection. The calendar shows several things that follow a first round, listed below. Whether a master's student can use them after missing a round, and in what order, is `TODO(verify)`.

1. **Don't wait.** Open `calendar-115.md` and find the next window that is still open or coming up (for semester 1, the second all-school add/drop was 2026-09-07 to 2026-09-14).
2. **Contact the department office and Academic Affairs** as soon as you notice. `templates/office-email-missed-selection.md` has a short email in English and Mandarin.
3. **Ask specifically:** which window can I still use, is my course full, do I need a signed form, and what is the last day.
4. **Plan the next round.** Put the next first-round dates into your calendar (`dist/wenzao-115-masters.ics`): semester 2 opens 2026-12-21, and the round for the next academic year opens 2027-05-31.

## Choosing courses (general advice)

- Look at the department's course and credit table first (links in `departments/gies.md` and `departments/dia.md`), then count what you still need (`TODO(verify)`: the mandatory course list for your cohort).
- Check which courses are taught in English before the window opens, and which have prerequisites or caps.
- Put the thesis-writing credits into your plan for the semester they are meant to be taken (`TODO(verify)` when).
- If a course is full, ask the instructor about it in the add/drop window instead of waiting.
