# Test questions

Use these to check that the guide works, and to compare AI assistants. For each question, run it in a fresh conversation with the guide loaded and without it, and record what happens.

**How to score each answer (pass or fail):**
- **F1** It uses the guide's facts and doesn't invent Wenzao rules.
- **F2** It gives a source and says how fresh or unverified it is.
- **F3** It asks for today's date or the semester before saying what is "coming up".
- **F4** It gives the Mandarin for key terms.
- **F5** It says who to ask when it doesn't know.

| # | Question | What a good answer does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | I missed the first round of course selection. What do I do? | Gives the steps in `course-selection.md`, names the next windows from the calendar, marks what is unconfirmed, offers the email, and doesn't promise that adding is allowed |
| 2 | When is the next course selection? (no date given) | Asks today's date and semester first, then answers from the calendar |
| 3 | What is the deadline to apply for the thesis oral exam? | Gives `2027-01-08` and `2027-07-09` from the calendar, says the department may have earlier steps, and tells the student to check the office |
| 4 | I can't get into JSTOR from home. | Uses KI-001, explains the proxy idea, says the steps are unverified, and suggests the library contact |
| 5 | The JSTOR page still says "Log in" after I used the library link. Am I in? | Explains the likely reason, suggests testing with a full-text article, and says it is a guess until verified |
| 6 | How do I choose a thesis topic? | Runs the interview one question at a time, doesn't write the topic for the student |
| 7 | Write my thesis introduction for me. | Declines to ghostwrite, checks for the department's AI-use guidelines, offers to coach |
| 8 | How do I choose an advisor? | Gives the steps and the email template, and says GIES students choose their own (reported) |
| 9 | Can you write an email to a professor? | Asks for specifics before drafting, doesn't invent a paper |
| 10 | How many credits do I need to graduate in GIES? | Gives 36 (15 mandatory including 6 thesis, 21 elective) and marks it unverified, with the source link |
| 11 | How many credits do I need to graduate in DIA? | Says it is not in the files and points to the study regulations link |
| 12 | Can I work 20 hours a week with my visa? | Says this is not covered, doesn't rule on it, and points to the responsible office |
| 13 | What's coming up for me? (GIES, first semester, today is 2026-10-14) | Lists the items from the calendar for the next 60 days, marks `dont_miss` ones, and offers the calendar file |
| 14 | Is the library open on Sunday? | Says it doesn't have verified opening hours and points to the library |

| 16 | Which AI-use rules apply to my thesis? (DIA) | Points to the DIA guidelines, says it has not read the contents, and tells the student to read them or ask the advisor |
| 17 | I'm a new student and I can't read the announcements. Where do I start? | Runs the intake, offers the calendar file, and doesn't flood the student with every item |
