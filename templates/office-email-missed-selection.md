# Email to the office: I missed a course selection round

Send it to the department office, and copy or send a similar message to the Academic Affairs Office (教務處). Have a Mandarin speaker check the Mandarin version before it is sent.

## English

Subject: Missed course selection round: [name], [department], student ID [number]

Dear [department] office,

I am [name], an international master's student in [department] (student ID [number]). I missed the [first-round / add-drop] online course selection for [semester] on [date window] because [one-sentence reason, for example: I did not know the dates because the announcement was in Mandarin].

Could you tell me:
1. Which course selection window I can still use, and until when?
2. Whether I need a form or a signature, and where to submit it?
3. Whether [course name and code, if you know it] still has space?

I can come to the office on [days and times]. Thank you for your help.

Best regards,
[name]
[email, phone]

## 中文
