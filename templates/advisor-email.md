# Email to a possible advisor

Keep it short. Replace everything in [brackets]. Have a Mandarin speaker check the Mandarin version before it is sent.

## English

Subject: Request for a short meeting about thesis supervision

Dear Professor [surname],

My name is [name], and I am a [first/second]-year international master's student in [department] (student ID [number]).

I am interested in [topic in one sentence]. I read [one specific paper, book, or course of theirs] and found [one specific thing] useful for my question: [your draft question].

Would you have 15 to 20 minutes in the coming weeks to talk about whether you might supervise my thesis? I can meet at [days and times], in [English / Mandarin].

Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
[name]
[email, phone]

## 中文

主旨：請教碩士論文指導事宜

[姓氏]老師您好：

我是[系所]碩士班[一/二]年級的國際學生[姓名]（學號[學號]）。

我對[研究主題]很有興趣，讀了您的[論文/著作/課程]，其中[具體內容]對我的研究問題「[研究問題]」很有幫助。

想請問老師近期是否方便給我 15 到 20 分鐘，討論是否有機會由您指導我的碩士論文？我可以配合[時間]，使用[英文/中文]溝通。

謝謝老師撥冗。

[姓名] 敬上
[Email、電話]

## Notes for the agent

- Ask the student for the specifics before filling the draft. A vague email gets a vague reply.
- Don't invent the paper or the student's interests.
