# Advisor meeting agenda

Send it to the advisor a day before. Keep it to half a page.

## First meeting

1. **Introduce:** who I am, my background, my languages (2 minutes).
2. **My topic:** my draft question and the worksheet (5 minutes).
3. **Their view:** is it answerable, feasible, and a fit for their expertise?
4. **Expectations:** ask about each of these.
   - How often will we meet?
   - What language do we use, and in writing?
   - How long does feedback usually take?
   - What does the timeline to the proposal look like, and what are the department's deadlines?
   - What do you expect me to bring to each meeting?
5. **Next step:** what I will do before we meet again, and the date.

## Later meetings

1. Since last time: what I did.
2. What I need help with: one or two concrete questions.
3. Feedback on the attached [outline / paragraph / sources].
4. Next steps and date.

## After the meeting

Send a two-line email: "Thank you. As discussed, I will [X] by [date]. Please let me know if I misunderstood anything."
